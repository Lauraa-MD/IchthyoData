# IchthyoData — Terms of Use

**Last updated: September 2026**

IchthyoData is a scientific platform developed by **Laura Modesti Donin**
for the integration, exploration, visualization, comparison, and export of
occurrence data of Neotropical fishes.

## 1. Use of IchthyoData

IchthyoData is made available for scientific, educational, and other
non-commercial purposes.

Users may use the publicly available platform to search, visualize, compare,
and export information obtained from the data sources integrated into
IchthyoData.

Use of the IchthyoData source code is subject to the terms described in the
LICENSE file distributed with the software.

Use of IchthyoData in scientific publications, reports, academic works,
presentations, datasets, or other technical and scientific products must
acknowledge and cite IchthyoData.

The recommended citation is provided in the `CITATION.cff` file associated
with the corresponding software version.

When a DOI is available for a released version of IchthyoData, users should
preferentially cite the DOI of the version used.

IchthyoData integrates and provides access to information obtained from

IchthyoData does not claim ownership of occurrence records, taxonomic
information, specimen information, geographic information, or other
third-party data retrieved through these sources.

The original data remain subject to the licenses, terms of use, attribution

Using IchthyoData does not replace the user's responsibility to identify,
verify, and appropriately cite the original data sources.

Specific information about the external sources integrated into IchthyoData
is provided in `DATA_SOURCES.md`.

## 4. Data quality and validation

IchthyoData facilitates the retrieval and integration of information but
does not independently validate every record returned by external sources.

Records may contain taxonomic, geographic, curatorial, transcription,
identification, georeferencing, or other errors originating from the
underlying data sources.

The presence of a record in IchthyoData should therefore not be interpreted
as independent validation of the taxonomic identification, geographic
occurrence, or other information associated with that record.

Users are responsible for assessing whether the data are appropriate for
their intended purpose.

Users are also responsible for complying with the licenses and citation

## 6. Availability and changes

Because IchthyoData relies partly on external databases, APIs, and online
services, the availability, structure, content, and number of records

External services may also become temporarily unavailable or modify their
APIs without prior notice.

For reproducibility, users are encouraged to record the version of
